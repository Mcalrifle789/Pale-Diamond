04 — Security

Your key stays
on our side of the wall.

The API key that reaches OpenRouter lives on the server and is never sent to the browser, never rendered into the page, and never recoverable from a session. Requests are signed, checked and rate limited against your plan before they leave.

Half of every payment funds that routing key. The other half is the company's. That split is applied at the moment of payment and recorded in a ledger you can audit.
